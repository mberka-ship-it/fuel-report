Strait of Hormuz: current situation

The June reopening improved crude flows, but July attacks and renewed hostilities prevented a normalisation of shipping or refined-product output.

As at 27 Jul 2026

Transit is partial and unsafe

Gulf exports recovered to 16.1 mb/d in June from the spring collapse, still far below the 24 mb/d pre-war average. Commercial traffic was reported at a three-week low on 27 July.

Products lag crude

In June, Gulf refined-product and LPG exports were still below half of pre-war levels while crude flows approached three-quarters. That gap explains the persistent diesel and petrol margin shock.

Bypass is limited

Saudi and UAE pipelines offer roughly 3.5–5.5 mb/d of available bypass capacity. Bab el-Mandeb threats now also weaken the Red Sea alternative.

What would change the outlook?

Improve

Durable ceasefire, safe-passage assurance, lower war-risk insurance and restart of Gulf export refineries.

Current

Intermittent protected transits, alternative-route exports and continuing emergency stock releases.

Deteriorate

More merchant-ship attacks, mine risk, Lytton outage, or disruption of Saudi Red Sea bypass routes.

Australia’s exposure and substitutes

Direct Gulf barrels are not the whole story. Australia mainly buys finished fuels from Asian refineries whose crude supply and product margins remain tied to the Gulf.

Method, uncertainty and sources

Observed figures and model assumptions are deliberately separated.

SEQ stock availability: what is known

Commercial Brisbane/SEQ tank-by-tank inventory is not publicly reported. Official MSO figures are national and include qualifying stocks in Australia and in the Australian EEZ.

The model therefore uses national cover as a planning proxy, not a measurement of local tanks. SEQ daily demand is estimated from Queensland trailing-12-month sales: 70% for petrol and jet, while diesel is anchored to IOR’s statement that its 110 ML Lytton terminal represents up to 12 days of Brisbane-market supply.

Uncertainty: medium for demand; low-to-medium for the 4–8 week direction; low for any precise local stock volume. Cargo allocation, terminal utilisation and refinery product yields are commercial data.

Refresh design

The page loads data/snapshot.json. A scheduled GitHub Action fetches the official Fuel Plan page weekly and updates national stocks, arrivals, prices and Queensland retail outages. The geopolitical assessment and APS demand assumptions remain reviewed snapshots because automated narrative updates would be unsafe.

If an automated refresh fails, the last validated snapshot remains visible rather than substituting missing or invented values.
